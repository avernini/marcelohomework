Step 3: Grammar Focus (10-12 min)

Asking Questions with DO (I, You, We, They)

When using action verbs, we use the helper word do for pronouns I, you, we, they:

Structure: Wh- + DO + (I/you/we/they) + Verb?

Building routine questions:

• Where do you work?

• What do you do?

• When do we start?

• Where do they live?

Answering with Action Verbs

Responding directly:

• I work downtown.

• We start at 9 AM.

• They live in São Paulo.

Fossilized Error Guard (Precision Check)
❌ COMMON MISTAKE

"Where you work?" (forgetting the helper word 'do')

"Where are you work?" (mixing action verbs with 'to be')

🟢 CORRECT STRUCTURE

"Where do you work?"

"What do you do?"

Interactive Grammar Training (4 Challenges)

1. "______ do you work?" (Asking for location)

2. "Where ______ you work?"

3. "______ do you do?" (Asking for occupation)

4. "When ______ we start work?"
