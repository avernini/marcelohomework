Step 3: Grammar Calibration (10-12 min)

Grammar Focus: Third Person Rules (-s, -es, -ies) & Frequency Adverbs Placement

For He, She, and It, we add -s to verbs. When adding frequency adverbs, place them before the main verb:

1. Verb Endings Rules

• Standard: work -> works

• -ch/sh/ss/x/o: watch -> watches

• Consonant + y: study -> studies

2. Frequency Adverbs

• He usually works on Saturdays.

• She never drinks soda.

• (Before main verb, after verb 'to be').
