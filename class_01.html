Step 1: Scenario Hook & Warm-up (5-8 min)

Casual Greetings & Daily Conversation

Welcome to your very first English class! Today we will learn how native speakers actually greet each other using How's it going? and How are you doing?, plus how to talk about your day using in the morning, in the afternoon, and at night. Let's start with our warm-up chat:

Warm-up Questions (A1 Friendly - 5 Questions to Talk with Your Teacher)

💬 1. Quando você encontra um amigo na rua, o que você costuma falar para cumprimentá-lo em português?

💬 2. Você já conhece alguma palavra em inglês que aparece em filmes (ex: Hello, Bye, OK, Coffee)?

💬 3. Você toma café de manhã (in the morning) ou prefere à noite (at night)?

💬 4. Você costuma trabalhar ou estudar in the afternoon (à tarde)?

💬 5. Se você pudesse estalar os dedos e falar inglês fluentemente amanhã, qual seria a primeira coisa que você faria com essa habilidade?

NATURAL DIALOGUE · CASUAL GREETINGS

Alex: Hey! How's it going?

Sam: Hi Alex! Everything is good. How are you doing?

Alex: I'm great! Do you drink coffee in the morning?

Sam: Yes, I do! But I don't drink coffee at night.

Step 3: Grammar Focus (10-12 min)

Sentences & Casual Conversation

Let's master how sentences work with everyday habits and how to respond to casual greetings:

1. Affirmative (+)

Stating actions:

• I work in the morning.

• I'm doing great, thanks!

2. Negative (-)

Using "don't":

• I don't work at night.

• I don't drink tea.

3. Question (?)

Starting with "Do":

• How are you doing?

• Do you work in the afternoon?

Fossilized Error Guard (Precision Check)
❌ COMMON MISTAKE

"How you go?" or "How is going?" (incorrect greeting grammar)

"I work in the night." (wrong preposition)

🟢 CORRECT STRUCTURE

"How's it going?" or "How are you doing?"

"I work at night."

Interactive Sentence Training (4 Challenges)

1. "______'s it going?" (Casual Greeting)

2. "How are you ______?"

3. "I drink coffee ______ the morning."

4. "Do you study ______ night?"
