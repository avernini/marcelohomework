Reforço de Fixação: Wh- Questions e Present Simple

Complete os blocos de exercícios práticos abaixo para consolidar o uso de pronomes interrogativos (com foco especial em Which para escolhas específicas), negações com don't e a estrutura central de perguntas no Present Simple. Cada bloco contém 5 desafios.

Exercise 1: Mastering Which & Wh- Questions

5 Questions

1. "______ laptop do you prefer? The Apple or the Dell?" (Choosing from a set)

2. "______ do you usually work during the week? Downtown or from home?"

3. "______ do we start our team meetings? At 9 AM?"

4. "______ project do you want to select? Project A or Project B?"

5. "______ do you talk to when you need IT assistance?"

Exercise 2: Negative Sentences (Don't + Verb)

5 Questions

1. I ______ like working in a noisy environment.

2. We ______ use paper archives anymore in our office.

3. They ______ review contracts on weekends.

4. You ______ need to bring your laptop to this meeting.

5. I ______ agree with that strategy proposal.

Exercise 3: Question Helpers & Structure (Do)

5 Questions

1. "______ you prefer the desktop or the laptop?"

2. "Where ______ your colleagues buy their office supplies?"

3. "______ you take your laptop to every meeting?"

4. "What software ______ we use for tracking tasks?"

5. "______ you want to review the report now?"
